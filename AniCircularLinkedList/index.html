🎓 What is Teaching Mode?

Teaching Mode is for the classroom. When it is On:

  • At key statements an “Ask the class” question appears — for example: “Where does tail.next point in a circular list?”
  • The answer stays hidden until you press Reveal Answer — ask your students first!
  • During Play, the animation pauses automatically at every question.

When it is Off, the animation runs step by step without questions.
